<h2 align="center">✧ &nbsp;About Me&nbsp; ✧</h2>

Most businesses don't have a data problem. They have a **trust problem**. The numbers in GA4 don't match Shopify, the ad platforms each claim credit for the same sale, and the dashboard everyone was excited about quietly stopped being opened three months ago.

That gap is where I work.

I'm a freelance **Data & Analytics Engineer** and a Computer Systems Engineering graduate from **NED University of Engineering & Technology**. I partner with ecommerce brands, agencies and service businesses to design the full measurement stack: from the moment a user clicks an ad, through the events that capture what they do, into a warehouse where that data gets cleaned and joined, and finally onto a report that answers the question someone actually asked.

I care about the unglamorous parts: naming conventions, deduplication, timezone handling, what happens when an API returns nothing at 3am. Those details are the difference between a dashboard that looks good in a demo and one a business can run on.
